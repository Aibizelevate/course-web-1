About

The person behind this

Martin Krejci

Co-Founder, Xroad Studio

Martin Krejci didn't start his career in AI. After moving from the Czech Republic to Canada, he spent years building and operating businesses across very different industries: rental vehicles, construction, CNC machining, and technology for the oil & gas sector.

That experience taught him something that became even more obvious as technology evolved: most businesses don't have a technology problem. They have a time problem.

Today, Martin works with AI, automation, and business operations, helping business owners find the repetitive work, bottlenecks, and processes quietly consuming their team's time, and turn them into practical solutions.

This session brings that experience together into a simple, step-by-step approach you can apply directly to your own business.
